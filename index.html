What This Program Stands For

The book and the boot, both looked after.

A player's passion for the game and a player's education are not competing priorities here — they're both non-negotiable. The Foundation is built so players keep chasing the game hard, while coaches, teachers, and guardians can trust that nothing else is being sacrificed to do it.

The Journey

Nobody starts in the first team.

Every session in The Foundation is built on ground Cantona has actually walked — not theory borrowed from somewhere else.

2018

First boots, fresh from high school

Picked up rugby straight out of school — no academy, no shortcut, just the start of it.

2018 – 2023

Masinde Muliro University — Biochemistry & the university team

Studied Biochemistry while working up through the university rugby ranks, reaching the main team by the third and fourth years — proof the books and the boots can share a schedule.

Year One at Kabras

Second team, first cameos

Arrived at Kabras RFC to a squad full of players who'd worn the national jersey. Spent the first season earning minutes, not given them — closing the year with 43 points.

Years Two & Three

A starting jersey, then the top of the table

Sealed a starting position, nearly doubling his output to 99 points in year two. In the third season, started 13 of 14 games and finished as the league's top point scorer with 133 points. Those campaigns also brought team silverware — Kenya Cup and Enterprise Cup titles alongside the individual accolades.

Building The Foundation

Turning that road into a program — so the next player climbing it doesn't have to figure out every step alone.

The Program

Four things every young player needs — and rarely gets taught together.

01 — On the field

Rugby skills & technique

Core technical work — handling, positioning, decision-making — built from match-level experience, not a textbook.

02 — Between the ears

Mindset & discipline

What it actually takes to go from the second team to a starting jersey — told straight, with the setbacks left in.

03 — Off the field

Education & career, alongside the game

Education isn't set aside for rugby here — it's carried alongside it. Practical guidance on keeping a degree or schoolwork moving at the same pace as the game, drawn from actually doing both at once.

04 — The engine

Fitness & conditioning

Foundational conditioning principles a young player can actually keep up between sessions.
